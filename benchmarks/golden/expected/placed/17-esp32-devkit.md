## Notes

- Tier 4. An ESP32-DevKitC (38 pins) reads a DS18B20 on IO15 (4.7k pull-up), lights an LED on IO2, reads a button on IO4 (10k pull-up) and drives an SSD1306 OLED on I2C (IO21/IO22).
- The DevKitC isn't in KiCad's library, so it is a 38-pin DIP with its pin rows 1.0 in apart. It covers a whole breadboard's width, so it sits across two boards snapped together long side to long side, one row of pins in each. Pin 1 is 3V3, counting down the left row.
