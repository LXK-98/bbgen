## Notes

- Tier 2. GPIO16 drives a PN2222A through 1k; the transistor switches the relay coil from VBUS, a 1N4148 across the coil catches the flyback. An LED shows the coil is on.
- KiCad's PN2222A footprint (TO-92_Inline) is 1.27 mm pitch, off the grid: TO-92_Inline_Wide instead.
- Relay: a Standex Meder DIP reed relay (SPST, closing), which sits across the trench like a DIP-14. Coil on pins 2 and 6, contacts on 1 (and 14) and 7 (and 8). (An Omron G5V-1's pin rows are 0.2 in apart: on a breadboard two of its pins always share a strip.)
