## Notes

- Tier 4. From ipod-classic-reverse-engineering docs/BREADBOARD.md 3a-3b: two LDOs and a buffered, trimmable reference, on the SMD-to-DIP adapters (SOT-23-5 pad 5 ASSUMED on adapter pin 6). M2 adds 3c-3e: three SN74LVC8T245 level-shift the 18 data lanes, D/C, /WR and /CS from 3V3 to VDDIO, an SN74LVC1T45 shifts TE up to GP27, and P30/P32 get 10k pull-ups to VDDIO.
- ASSUMED: the TSSOP-24 parts sit on 0.6 in wide DIP-24 adapters, pad n on pin n. The circuit is too big for one board, so it takes two.
